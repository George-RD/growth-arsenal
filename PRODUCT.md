# growth-arsenal product context

## Product

growth-arsenal is an open-source collection of business growth workshops that run inside AI coding agents. It helps a founder or operator turn a rough business idea into a stronger offer, a concrete lead-generation system and plain customer-facing copy.

## Audience and job

The primary audience is builders, founders, consultants and small-business operators who already use an AI coding agent and want more structure and pushback than a normal chat provides.

The job is to expose weak assumptions before a real customer does, then leave the user with decisions and materials they can act on.

## Unique mechanism

- Phase-gated workshops rather than one long prompt.
- Independent adversarial agents review each phase in parallel.
- Dynamic customer personas are built from research and evolve through the workshop.
- Shared concerns become must-fix issues before a phase passes.
- Approved work is written to persistent Markdown and HTML files.
- The offer, lead-generation and copy skills hand work to one another.

## Shipped workshops

- `grandslam-offer`: market, pricing, value equation, offer stack, guarantee and naming.
- `hundred-million-leads`: lead magnets, channel choice, scripts, referrals and Rule of 100 plan.
- `business-copy-style`: plain-language, de-AI and adversarial reader checks for customer-facing copy.
- `plugin-feedback`: a progressive-disclosure end-of-workshop feedback flow. It is supporting infrastructure, not a primary marketed workshop.

## Constraints and honest limits

- This is not proof of demand and does not replace talking to real customers.
- Dynamic personas are a pressure test, not real buyers.
- Do not invent customer counts, performance benchmarks, revenue claims or affiliations.
- The work is inspired by Alex Hormozi's `$100M Offers` and `$100M Leads`; it is an independent implementation.
- The repository is MIT licensed.

## Voice

Direct, practical and plain. British spelling. Short sentences where useful, but not artificial fragments. No em dashes, inflated claims, generic AI phrases or fake urgency.

Install the repository or inspect it on GitHub.
